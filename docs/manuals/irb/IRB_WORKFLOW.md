# IRB Workflow

Submitting a human-subjects research study at Northwestern University may require multiple rounds of review. Following the workflow and recommendations below can help streamline the process and reduce unnecessary back-and-forth.

## Where to Submit

To initiate an IRB submission, log in to the [eIRB+ system](https://irb.northwestern.edu/submitting-to-the-irb/eirb/). If you are off campus, remember to use the off-campus access option provided on the website.

## What to Submit: Documents and File Organization

Before preparing your submission:

1. Review the IRB [checklists and templates](https://irb.northwestern.edu/resources-guidance/). The IRB office has strict formatting requirements, so use the appropriate template for each document and include every required section. Otherwise, you may be asked to revise and resubmit your materials.
2. Include a version date in every filename and in the corresponding portal entry. You may need to submit multiple versions of the same document during the review process.
3. Keep details consistent across all sections and documents. 

## Prerequisite: CITI Training

You must complete the required CITI training before submitting through eIRB+.

If you have already completed CITI training through Northwestern, you can skip this section. If you have not completed the training—or need to transfer training records from another institution—follow these steps to ensure that your CITI record appears in your eIRB+ profile:

1. Sign in to CITI through Northwestern University Single Sign-On (SSO), using the same Northwestern NetID that you use for eIRB+.
2. Follow the option that applies to you:

   - **If you have not completed the required training:** Complete it through the [CITI Program](https://about.citiprogram.org/).
   - **If you completed CITI training at another institution:** Sign in through that institution and add Northwestern University as an affiliated institution. Eligible course records may transfer automatically.

3. Check your CITI profile and confirm that the **Institutional Username** field contains your Northwestern NetID. If the field is blank, you have not yet signed in through Northwestern SSO.
4. Confirm that your training record appears in eIRB+. Emailed certificates are not accepted; your record must be linked and transferred electronically through CITI.
