# What is Bluesky?

Bluesky is a social media platform, an alternative to Twitter and Facebook. In fact, it was spun out of Twitter, and the app UI is basically the same as using Twitter. It's a platform that the lab uses to get social media data, as Twitter has become increasingly prohibitive for academic research. The lab has published a few papers that have used data from Bluesky.

## Who are the points of contact when working with Bluesky data?

The people in the lab with the most experience with working with Bluesky data are Billy, Mark, and Nick. Each of them can walk you through what's available on Bluesky and how it can be used for research.
